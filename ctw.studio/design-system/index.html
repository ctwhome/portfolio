05 / Evidence semantics

Name what kind of knowing this is.

Observation
Measured condition in defined population and period.

Useful for “what happened?” Never recast as cause without causal evidence.

Association
Variables move together under stated method.

Useful for investigation. Confounding and direction remain unresolved.

Experiment
Bounded intervention with comparison.

Useful for causal claims inside studied setting. Scale and transfer need separate evidence.

Forecast
Modelled future under assumptions.

Show horizon, baseline, interval, and model update date.

Source contract

Netherlands, households, 2024 Q4, percentage points. Observation. Revised 14 February 2026. Link to official table and method note.

Also preserve distinct labels for exposure, scenario, counterfactual, judgment, and hypothesis. Scope, period, population, denominator, geography, and unit travel with every public number.

06 / Expressive compositions

Editorial signal, measured like an instrument.

Direction is 70% Editorial Signal, 20% Research Instrument, 10% Kinetic Studio. Load compositions.css after tokens and components, then opt in beneath .ctw-scope.

Use bright amber, cyan, coral, or violet accents as structure on coal surfaces. Chalk maps spectral accents to its accessible action role unless a separately verified dark accent token exists. Add depth through overlap, crop, inset, stacked rules, offset planes, full-bleed media, or sticky chapter labels. No gradients, glass, soft shadow stacks, or generic bento layouts.

06 / Accessibility

Access is component behavior.

  • Skip link reaches one main landmark.
  • Keyboard order follows reading order; focus outline is 3px with 3px offset.
  • Normal text reaches 4.5:1 contrast; large text and meaningful boundaries reach 3:1.
  • Compact touch targets are at least 44 by 44px.
  • Labels persist above fields; errors use text and aria-describedby.
  • Tables have captions, scoped headers, and labelled scroll regions.
  • At 200% zoom, content reflows without loss or two-dimensional page scrolling.
  • Reduced-motion preference removes non-essential transition and animation.
  • Essential content and route guidance remain complete without JavaScript.
Try it. Press Tab from browser chrome. Skip link appears first; every control receives a strong amber outline.

07 / Do + don't

Keep signal. Remove costume.

System exists to reduce invention, not editorial character.

Do

Lead with consequential question.

Expose evidence type, boundary, source, and action. Use rules, spacing, and type to reveal structure. Keep bright spectral accents on coal; let chalk use its accessible action role.

Don't

Imitate generic dashboard.

Don't place bright coal-surface accents on chalk unless a separately verified dark accent token exists. No fake metrics, glass panels, ornamental gradients, floating-card clutter, generic three-card heroes, or filler claims.

10 / Migration checklist

Finish one boundary at a time.

  1. Name family owner, Decide/Learn intent, theme, and controlled accent.
  2. Load tokens.css, then components.css. Load compatibility aliases only when approved.
  3. Add .ctw-scope at smallest useful boundary.
  4. Replace repeated raw values with role tokens. Keep data/topic variables local.
  5. Use landmarks, ordered headings, links, buttons, labels, captions, and tables before adding classes.
  6. Verify hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, invalid, empty, loading, and long-content states that route uses.
  7. Test keyboard, contrast, 200% zoom, reduced motion, no-JS, compact, and wide layouts.
  8. Run design-system tests and affected family tests; confirm generated and unrelated surfaces remain untouched.
